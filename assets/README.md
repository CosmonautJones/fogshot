# Art provenance — moonlit ridgeline

`night-ridge.svg` is original Fogshot artwork authored for this repository on 2026-10-14. The SVG source is the editable master: gradients, moon, static public ridges, fixed distant relay landmark, grain and ground rim. No third-party image pack, reference-game artwork, logo, generated text image, external font or traced commercial asset is included.

`field.css` and the launcher/core/material/effect drawing in `client/mount_field.js` are also original presentation work. Bodies are drawn only from the server's entitled poses and clipped dimensions. The landscape is fixed public scenery, independent of match layouts; its ridges are distant decoration. The collision surface remains flat at world y=0.

Claude Code supplied a bounded, tool-disabled design critique. Native output metadata reported `claude-sonnet-5-5`; no asset or gameplay authority was delegated. Persistent scorch decals and ambient movement were deliberately omitted. Gemini was not available as a CLI or callable tool, so no Gemini asset is claimed.

No third-party art attribution is required for these original assets. This does not select a distribution license for the whole repository; that owner decision remains open. Phaser, React and the other pinned dependencies retain their upstream licenses. No dependency license is being used to claim rights to external game art.

The 1600×800 SVG is rasterized once to an 800×400 texture by Phaser. Fog texture generation happens once per mount. Optional effects are bounded to 10 dots and one ring for 0.8 seconds, with no emitters, texture downloads or full-scene camera effects. Reduced motion omits the pulse/dots and projectile glow; authoritative projectile and collapse playback remains visible.
